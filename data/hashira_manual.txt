Hashira Training Manual (leaked from the Corps archive)

Every new slayer must first learn to breathe. Total concentration breathing fills the body with oxygen, sharpens the senses and makes the blood run hot enough to keep pace with a demon. A slayer who loses control of the breath loses the fight long before the blade is drawn.

Demons cannot stand sunlight, and they can only truly die when the head is cut with a Nichirin blade. The steel for these blades is mined on a mountain that faces the sun all year, which is why every blade changes colour when its owner first draws it.

The Kasugai crow assigned to each slayer is more than a messenger. It carries orders from headquarters, warns of demon sightings and reports when a slayer falls. A slayer should treat the crow as a partner, because in the field the crow is often the only link to help.

Training on the mountain follows a strict routine. Recruits run before dawn, practise swings until their arms shake, and spend the evening on breathing exercises while carrying heavy loads. The aim is not strength alone but the habit of keeping the breath steady when the body is exhausted.

A slayer must learn to read the enemy before striking. Each demon has a blood demon art with its own pattern, and most of them repeat that pattern under pressure. Watching for the repeat and striking in the gap is safer than trading blows at full strength.

Lower ranked demons often leave a trail of disappearances in villages near forests and mountain roads. Scouts should compare reports from several villages, because the same small detail repeated in different places usually points to a single demon moving along a route.

The manual sets three rules for every mission. Never fight alone at night if a partner is within reach. Report every sighting to the crow before engaging. Retreat to a lit place when the breath is failing, since a living slayer can return to finish the job.

The Corps survives because knowledge is passed from one generation of slayers to the next. Every technique in this manual was paid for by someone who did not come home, and every recruit owes it to them to study it with care.
