Smell has always helped me more than sight. A demon carries a sour, rotten scent that stays in the air long after it has gone, and following that scent has saved me more than once.

My sister was the reason I joined. I promised her that no other family in our village would wake up to an empty house, and that promise is heavier than my sword.

I think the Corps should spend more time teaching recruits how to work in pairs. Most of us trained alone on a mountain, and we only learned to cover each other after a mission went badly.

If I could change one thing, I would build more wisteria houses along the mountain roads, so that injured slayers always have a safe place within an hour's walk.
