I am not as fast or as gifted as the others, so I study harder. I keep a notebook of every demon I have seen and the mistakes I made against each one.

My teacher used to say that a calm mind is sharper than any blade. I did not believe him until the day I panicked, missed an easy cut and had to run for my life.

Rice balls taste better after a mission than at any other time. Food, sleep and a few kind words from friends are what make it possible to go out again the next night.

The first night after Final Selection I could not sleep at all. Every sound in the forest felt like footsteps, and I kept my hand on the sword until the sky turned grey.
