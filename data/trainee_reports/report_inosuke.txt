Wild boars taught me more than any teacher. They charge straight, never hesitate and never apologise, and I fight the same way whether the manual likes it or not.

Training on the mountain follows a hard routine. Recruits run before sunrise, practise swings until their arms tremble, and spend every evening on breathing drills while carrying heavy rocks. The goal is not just power but the habit of keeping the breath steady when the body is worn out.

Low ranked demons usually leave a trail of missing people in villages near forests and mountain paths. Scouts should compare reports from many villages, because the same small detail repeated in different places mostly points to one demon travelling along a route.

Rice balls taste better after a mission than at any other time. Food, sleep and a few kind words from friends are what make it possible to go out again the next night.
